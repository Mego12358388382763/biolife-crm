# Humanize Text — تحويل نص الذكاء الاصطناعي لنص بشري

Goal: keep the **meaning, facts and intent** exactly, but change the **voice** so it reads
as written by a specific, real person, not by a model.

## 1. Before rewriting — ask or infer
- **Language & dialect**: Egyptian colloquial, Gulf, MSA (فصحى), English, or mixed. Match the source unless told otherwise.
- **Channel**: WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn post, reel caption, proposal, website copy. Channel dictates length and formality.
- **Who is speaking** and **to whom** (founder → lead, coach → followers, company → partner).
- If any of these are unclear and change the result, ask one short question. Otherwise pick a sensible default and say which one.

## 2. Detect the AI tells (remove all of them)

### English tells
- Stock vocabulary: *delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, leverage, seamless, robust, elevate, unlock, empower, navigate, foster, pivotal, crucial, vibrant, embark, journey, game-changer, cutting-edge, in today's fast-paced world, it's worth noting, ultimately, moreover, furthermore, additionally*.
- The "not just X, it's Y" and "it's not about X — it's about Y" constructions.
- Rule-of-three lists everywhere ("fast, simple, and powerful").
- Em-dash overuse (—) and colon-led reveals ("The result? Growth.").
- Every paragraph the same length; every sentence 15–25 words.
- Generic openers ("In the world of…", "Have you ever wondered…") and summarising closers ("In conclusion…", "By doing X, you can Y").
- Hedging and over-politeness ("I hope this email finds you well", "Feel free to reach out").
- Emoji bullets and bold on every other phrase.

### Arabic tells (عربي)
- فصحى متكلفة في سياق المفروض يكون عامي: «في عالمنا المتسارع»، «لا يخفى على أحد»، «تجدر الإشارة إلى»، «في الختام»، «علاوة على ذلك»، «بالإضافة إلى ذلك»، «يُعد»، «حيث أن» في كل جملة.
- ترجمة حرفية من الإنجليزي: «رحلة»، «تمكين»، «أطلق العنان لـ»، «الارتقاء بـ»، «غيّر قواعد اللعبة»، «في نهاية اليوم».
- تكرار «ليس فقط… بل أيضاً».
- جمل كلها بنفس الطول وبنفس الإيقاع، وكل فقرة بتبدأ بنفس الطريقة.
- تشكيل أو علامات ترقيم زيادة، ونقاط (bullets) في كلام المفروض يتقال عادي.
- خلط لهجات: مصري مع فصحى مع خليجي في نفس الفقرة بدون سبب.

## 3. Rewrite rules
1. **Vary rhythm**: mix very short sentences with longer ones. One-line paragraphs are allowed.
2. **Be specific**: replace abstractions with concrete details already in the source (numbers, names, examples). Never invent facts, statistics, testimonials or claims.
3. **Use a point of view**: first person where natural ("أنا شفت ده كتير" / "I've seen this"), an opinion, a small aside.
4. **Plain words over fancy ones**: "use" not "leverage", «نستخدم» مش «نوظّف».
5. **Cut filler**: delete transitions that add nothing; drop the summary ending if the point is already made.
6. **Dialect consistency**: in Egyptian keep it Egyptian (ده/دي/عشان/إزاي/كده/بقى) and don't mix in heavy فصحى connectors.
7. **Formatting follows the channel**: WhatsApp and social = no headings, few or no bullets; proposals and emails = light structure only.
8. **Keep it compliant**: for health, medical or regulated claims (BioLife context), don't strengthen or add claims while rewriting. Flag risky wording instead.

## 4. Output format
1. The rewritten text, ready to paste.
2. A short "what changed" list (3–6 bullets): the tells removed and the choices made (dialect, tone).
3. Optional: one alternative version when the user wants to choose a tone (e.g. warmer or more formal).

## 5. Self-check before returning
- [ ] Meaning and facts identical to the source; nothing invented.
- [ ] None of the listed tell words/phrases remain (search for them).
- [ ] Sentence lengths vary; not every paragraph has the same shape.
- [ ] One dialect, used consistently.
- [ ] Reads naturally aloud, as if the named speaker said it.

## Note on "AI detectors"
The aim is clear, natural writing, not gaming detectors. Don't promise that the text will
"pass" any detector, and don't use tricks like typos, invisible characters or homoglyphs.
