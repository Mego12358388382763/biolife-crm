# Health data privacy

Health data is sensitive personal data; mishandling it can hurt clients and the business. This skill gives practical guidance, not legal advice — for binding answers, recommend a lawyer.

## Review checklist

1. **Minimization**: does the CRM/form need this field? Sales CRM notes should avoid diagnoses, medications and lab results unless there's a clear need and consent.
2. **Consent**: clear consent for contact (WhatsApp/email) and separate explicit consent for any health info and for testimonials.
3. **Purpose**: data used only for what the person agreed to; no adding form sign-ups to broadcasts without consent.
4. **Access**: least privilege — CRM roles (read_only for viewers), no shared accounts, remove leavers promptly.
5. **Sharing**: no client lists in WhatsApp groups or personal drives; exports deleted after use; no pasting identifiable health data into tools unnecessarily.
6. **Retention & deletion**: how long data is kept; how a person can ask to be removed.
7. **Bought lists**: source and lawful basis for contacting them.
8. **Breach plan**: who does what if data leaks.

Check current details of Law 151/2020 and its executive regulations via web search when specifics matter, and cite them.

## Output

Findings ranked (high/medium/low), each with a concrete fix, plus ready-to-use consent text for forms and WhatsApp in Egyptian Arabic.
