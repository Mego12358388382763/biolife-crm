# Competitor watch

1. **List**: agree on 5–10 competitors (direct and adjacent). If none given, find them via web search and ask Ahmed to confirm.
2. **Per competitor collect** (public info only): offer and prices, target audience, main promise/positioning, content themes and formats that perform, ads (Meta Ad Library), reviews/complaints (Google Maps, social comments), partnerships, recent changes.
3. **Compare** in one table vs BioLife.
4. **Insights**:
   - gaps they leave (unserved audience, unanswered questions, trust issues in reviews)
   - claims they make that BioLife should not (over-promising) — an opportunity to win on honesty
   - content ideas to answer, not copy
   - pricing/offer implications
5. Date everything; offer a monthly repeat.

Ethics: public information only; no fake accounts, scraping behind logins, or copying their content.
