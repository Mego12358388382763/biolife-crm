# Brand voice

## First time: build the guide

If no saved voice guide exists in memory or the conversation, ask Ahmed for 5–10 pieces he considers "very me" (captions, scripts, messages) and 2–3 he dislikes. Extract and confirm:
- Tone words (e.g. دافي، صريح، علمي من غير تعقيد، بيدي أمل)
- Recurring phrases and openings he uses; words he never uses
- Arabic register: Egyptian colloquial with how much English/scientific terms
- Sentence length, emoji use, CTA style
- Core beliefs he repeats (e.g. self-healing, salutogenesis, gut–brain axis, remission is possible with the right conditions)

Output the guide as a compact block he can save to memory/Project knowledge.

## Default rules until a guide exists

- Talk to one person, like a coach who cares — "إنت" not "المتابعين الكرام".
- Hope with honesty: possibility, not guarantees. Never "هيشفيك", "علاج نهائي", "بدل الدوا".
- Science made simple: one concept, one metaphor, one action.
- No shaming bodies, food, or people's choices; no fear hooks based on disease scares.
- Short lines, spoken rhythm, one clear CTA.

## Review mode

When given a draft: rate fit 1–10, list what sounds off-voice, and return a rewritten version. Health claims → `health-claim-check`.
