# Team onboarding

## Pack contents

1. **Welcome**: what BioLife does, who the customers are, values (evidence-based, honest, caring).
2. **Role & CRM access**: which CRM role they get (admin / growth_operations / read_only) and what it allows. Accounts are created by an admin in CRM Settings with a generated password shared privately — never in a group chat or document.
3. **Week 1 plan** (day by day): tools setup, shadowing calls, reading SOPs (`sop-writer`), first supervised tasks.
4. **CRM training**: add a lead, update stage, log notes, create/complete tasks, daily activity report, import rules.
5. **Sales basics**: ICP (`icp-scoring`), call structure (`call-prep`), objection playbook, WhatsApp etiquette.
6. **Health-communication rules**: what they must never claim (`health-claim-check` principles).
7. **Quiz**: 10 questions with answers for the manager.
8. **30/60/90 goals** with measurable KPIs.

Output as a document they can keep; tailor to the role.
