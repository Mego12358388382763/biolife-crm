# WhatsApp follow-up

WhatsApp is personal space: short, warm, respectful, never spammy.

## Rules

- 2–5 short lines. Greeting with name. One purpose. One easy question at the end.
- Egyptian Arabic, polite (حضرتك for seniors/doctors; أنت for peers only if the user says so).
- Reference context: where they met, what they asked, what was sent.
- Emojis: max one, only if the relationship is warm.
- No medical promises or cure claims — BioLife content must stay health-claim safe (`health-claim-check`).
- Respect opt-out: if they said not interested, one polite close and stop.

## Templates to produce on request

1. أول تواصل بعد ما حد شارك رقمه
2. فولو أب بعد يومين من غير رد
3. بعد مكالمة/ميتنج (ملخص + الخطوة الجاية)
4. إرسال عرض/أسعار
5. تذكير بميعاد
6. إعادة تواصل مع lead بارد بعد شهر+
7. رسالة برودكاست (لقائمة وافقت على الاستقبال)

Give 2 variants per message (أرسمي شوية / أودّ شوية). Suggest the next follow-up date so it can go into the CRM's `next_follow_up_at`.
