# Content calendar

## 1. Inputs

Month/week, platforms, posting frequency, current campaigns or launches (products, book, events, offers), and what performed well (from `social-analytics` if available).

## 2. Pillars (default; confirm with Ahmed)

1. تعليم — gut–brain axis, self-healing, salutogenesis concepts made simple
2. قصص وتجارب — client journeys (with consent), personal story
3. عملي — daily habits, food, sleep, stress tools
4. رد على خرافات — myth vs evidence (through `health-claim-check`)
5. بيزنس/عرض — BioLife offers, CTA «قدّم على جلستك» or the lead form (≤ 20%)

## 3. Plan

Table: date · platform · pillar · format (reel/carousel/story/post/live) · idea/hook · CTA · status. Mix formats; put the strongest ideas on the best days. Use Metricool `getBestTimeToPostByNetwork` for posting times when connected.

## 4. Produce & schedule

For each approved idea, generate content with `meaning-to-content` / `hook-factory` / `repurpose-engine`. Schedule in Metricool only after explicit approval — prefer `createScheduledPostForReview` so Ahmed approves inside Metricool; show the list of scheduled posts at the end.
