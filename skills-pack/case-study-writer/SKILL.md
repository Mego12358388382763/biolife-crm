# Case study writer

## Consent & privacy first

Health information is sensitive. Before writing, confirm: the client consented to this use and channel; whether they want their name/photo used; anonymize otherwise (change name, age range, city, remove identifying details). Never include diagnoses, medications or lab values without explicit consent.

## Structure

1. Who (anonymized) and where they started — in their own words when possible
2. What they tried before
3. What changed with BioLife — the process, not magic
4. What happened — specific, honest, in their words; mention other factors (doctor's treatment, lifestyle) where relevant
5. What they'd tell someone in their place
6. CTA

## Language rules

- "حست بتحسن في…" not "اتعالجت من…". Results are individual — add "النتايج بتختلف من شخص للتاني".
- No implication of stopping prescribed medication.
- Run through `health-claim-check`.

Outputs: short social version, long website/proposal version, 2–3 pull quotes.
