# Ad compliance

Rejected ads and restricted ad accounts cost money and reach. Health/wellness is a sensitive category.

## Check for

1. **Personal attributes**: copy that implies you know the viewer's condition ("عندك القولون العصبي؟", "Are you diabetic?") — Meta rejects this. Rewrite to general ("ناس كتير عندها مشاكل هضم…").
2. **Cure/guarantee claims**: "يعالج", "شفاء نهائي", "بدون أدوية", guaranteed results/timeframes.
3. **Before/after** images or body-shaming, unrealistic results.
4. **Negative self-perception** hooks.
5. **Medical claims** without evidence — run through `health-claim-check`.
6. **Supplements/products**: prohibited ingredients, disease treatment claims.
7. **Landing page** must match the ad and carry the same safe claims; include a disclaimer where relevant.
8. **Targeting**: no targeting by health condition in sensitive ways.

Policies change; if unsure about a current rule, check the platform's current ad policy page via web search and cite it.

## Output

Verdict (✅ safe / ⚠️ risky / ❌ likely rejected), each issue with the exact phrase and why, and a compliant rewrite of the full ad (primary text, headline, description, CTA) in Ahmed's voice.
